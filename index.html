Samuel Lowe

Mathematician, data scientist, and software engineer • Work recognized by the New York Times and National Public Radio

I’m Samuel Lowe (he/him), a specialist in platform work, full-stack web development, and data analytics. I’m currently based out of the greater Boston area, and consulting as a data analyst at the Center for Strategic Politics and as the lead software engineer for Oasis Management Inc. At CSP, I designed the weighted single transferable vote algorithm behind our 2025 New York City mayoral primary poll, which was featured in the New York Times. At Oasis, I lead a team of four other engineers building the Django employee portal and our mobile app.

I’m available for full-time or part-time work starting in January 2027. You can see more on my work experience page or in my resume, and reach me on LinkedIn or at sam@samlowe.dev.

I graduated from Northeastern University with a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics in 2023 and an M.S. in Applied Math in 2025, mostly focusing on number theory (specifically elliptic curves) and probability (specifically machine learning). Some of my projects, like nprcore.me, have been recognized by National Public Radio and All Things Considered. You can see more in my full portfolio and GitHub.
