Posted Jun 14, 2026 Updated Aug 10, 2026
By Samuel Lowe 1 min read

I’m a hobbyist game developer, and originally enrolled at Rochester Institute of Technology for Game Development. While I’m not currently in the games industry, I’m deeply invested in it as an artistic medium, and love studying game design. In addition to everything I’ve written about games, here’s some of the most insightful discussions I’ve seen. I don’t necessarily agree with every opinion expressed here, but they’re worth considering.
